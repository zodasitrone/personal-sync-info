Personal SyncPrivacy

Privacy information

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Overview. Personal Sync is operated by the account owner for use with their own Google account.

Data access and use. The utility accesses file contents and metadata, such as names and folder information, to carry out transfers and backups requested by the owner. It uses full Drive permission to work with existing files and folders.

Sharing. Files are transferred between Google Drive and systems controlled by the account owner. Data is not sold, used for advertising or disclosed to unrelated parties.

Personal Sync's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control. File copies and transfer logs, which may include file names and results, are kept until deleted. Access can be revoked through the Third-party connections section of Google Account settings. Revoking access does not delete existing copies or logs.

Contact. For questions, use the developer email provided during Google authorization.
